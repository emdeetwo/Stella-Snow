How to play
  • Press the snow at the bottom and drag to roll a snowball. It grows where there's snow.
  • Drop one snowball on another to stack. Three makes a snowman, and he stays.
  • Drag a snowman off the edge of the screen to say goodbye.
  • Tap the snowman to say hi. Tap him three times fast.
  • Snow piles on the windows. Drag one, or close it, and watch.
  • Tap the yeti to get him back. Tap a splat to wipe it off.
